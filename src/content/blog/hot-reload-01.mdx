On Saturday, October 17, we ran the first Hot Reload at Don Salazar Specialty Coffee in Miraflores, Lima. It happened thanks to [Vercel](https://vercel.com), who covered breakfast. Twenty spots, about fifteen people, and one rule: bring a side project to show or a question for the table.

I hosted it with Carlos Tarmeño. The full community recap is on the [Crafter Journal](https://crafter.run/en/blog/hot-reload-01-recap).

## What worked

The format. Once people started showing what they were building, nobody needed an agenda. We talked about our Crafter Station projects, and people were surprised by what we were building.

## The people

This is the part I keep thinking about.

At one table we met Diego Jesus and Antony Alvarez, political scientists from [DATAdaf](https://datadaf.com). They are now part of the Crafter Research crew.

At the same table was [Cristian Olivera](https://github.com/CristianOlivera1), creator of [Openvid](https://openvid.dev), which took first place at the [CubePath 2026 hackathon with midudev](https://cubepath.com/es/hackatones). I invited him to join the Crafter Station core team that morning. In less than a month he built [Muelle](https://github.com/crafter-station/Muelle), a macOS-style dock for Windows 11, and it passed 250 GitHub stars in its first week.

<Figure src="/images/blog/hot-reload-01/cristian-railly.webp" alt="Cristian Olivera and Railly Hugo at the Hot Reload table, with crafter.run open on the laptop" caption="Cristian and me at Hot Reload. For posterity." size="full" />

Three new crafters from one coffee. That is why I want to keep these small.

## What didn't

- Breaking the ice at the start took a while.
- Luma didn't ask for phone numbers, so we had to collect them one by one afterward.
- Some approved guests didn't show up, and with only twenty spots that hurts.
- Little promo and a small cap of twenty spots.
- The content depended on our own photos and Live Photos.
- We never tested the game at the event.
- We didn't measure anything: registrations, check-ins, shares.

## What's next

Edition 02 is on Saturday, December 12, in Lima, again with Vercel. What changes:

- A "bring your project" format: short pitches and one question for everyone, "how can we help?". The goal is partnerships.
- Physical stickers on the table to break the ice.
- Required phone numbers, room for about thirty people including staff, and a teaser built from this recap.
- Guests and check-in on our own [Makeables](https://makeables.dev) events.
- A hired photographer, a full rehearsal a week before, metrics from day one, and a recap in under 24 hours.

Register on [Luma](https://luma.com/hack0-5u0o).

Same day, we closed at AI Frontier Conf 26 at UTEC.
